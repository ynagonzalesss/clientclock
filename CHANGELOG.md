# Changelog

All notable changes to ClientClock are listed here.

## 1.0.1 – October 24, 2026

- New toolbar icon: the C-shaped clock now shows in Chrome's toolbar, matching the store listing
- Bigger, bolder icon so it's easier to spot at small sizes

## 1.0.0 – October 21, 2026

First release.

- See your own local time at a glance
- Add your clients' time zones and give each one a name
- Shows how many hours ahead or behind each client is, plus "Tomorrow" or "Yesterday" when the date differs
- Day and night look for each card, updated live
- Light, Dark, or Auto mode
- 12-hour or 24-hour clock
- Settings sync across your Chrome browsers
