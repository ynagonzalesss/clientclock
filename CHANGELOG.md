# Changelog

All notable changes to ClientClock are listed here.

## 1.1.0 – October 26, 2026

- Search by country, capital, major city, US state, Canadian province, or Australian state. No need to know the time zone name anymore (try "Nigeria", "Mumbai", "Arizona")
- Search by abbreviation too: EST, PST, CST, GMT, IST, WAT, AEST and more
- Modern place names: Kolkata, Kyiv, Ho Chi Minh City
- Results show friendly labels with the current time, like "Lagos, Nigeria · GMT+1 · 6:53 PM"
- Five color palettes: Blush, Sage, Slate, Sand, and Graphite, each with a light and dark look
- Five fonts for client names: Cormorant, Playfair, Fraunces, Dancing Script, and Outfit
- Client names are bolder and easier to read
- Softer sun on the main card in dark mode
- A one-time note shows existing users what's new

## 1.0.1 – October 24, 2026

- New toolbar icon: the C-shaped clock now shows in Chrome's toolbar, matching the store listing
- Bigger, bolder icon so it's easier to spot at small sizes

## 1.0.0 – October 21, 2026

First release.

- See your own local time at a glance
- Add your clients' time zones and give each one a name
- Shows how many hours ahead or behind each client is, plus "Tomorrow" or "Yesterday" when the date differs
- Day and night look for each card, updated live
- Light, Dark, or Auto mode
- 12-hour or 24-hour clock
- Settings sync across your Chrome browsers
