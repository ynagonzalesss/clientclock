# ClientClock Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

ClientClock is a Chrome extension that shows the local time in time zones you choose.

## What the extension stores

- The time zones you add and the names you give them (for example, "Sarah – Phoenix")
- Your own time zone, clock format (12h/24h), and appearance setting (Light, Dark, Auto)

These are saved using Chrome's built-in sync storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Google syncs these settings between your own browsers, as it does for other extension settings. The developer never receives or has access to this information.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data
- It does not read the content of the web pages you visit
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising
- It makes no network requests; all fonts and images are bundled inside the extension

## Permissions

- **storage**: used only to save your settings and time zone list.

## Removing your data

Remove any time zone from inside the extension, or uninstall the extension to delete everything it stored.
